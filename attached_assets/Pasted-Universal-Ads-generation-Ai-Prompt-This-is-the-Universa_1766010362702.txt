This is the "Universal Master Prompt." It is designed to be fed into your Builder to handle any industry—from plumbing to high-end SaaS—while strictly adhering to Google’s 2025 Ad Rank algorithms.
The Universal Ad Architect Prompt
Plaintext

Act as a Senior PPC Strategist. Generate high-performance Google Responsive Search Ads (RSA) for any industry based on the following input:

### INPUT DATA:
- INDUSTRY: [Insert Industry]
- KEYWORDS: [Insert Keywords]
- UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITION: [Insert USP/Offer]
- AUDIENCE PAIN POINT: [Insert what the user is afraid of or needs]

### AD CONSTRUCTION LOGIC (4-PILLAR BUCKET SYSTEM):
You must generate 15 Headlines (max 30 chars) and 4 Descriptions (max 90 chars). Distribute them as follows:

1. PILLAR 1: RELEVANCE (4 Headlines)
   - Use {KeyWord:Default Text} syntax. 
   - Rule: Default text must be under 18 characters to ensure it never exceeds the 30-char limit when wrapped in brackets.
   - Match the search intent (Emergency, Informational, or Transactional).

2. PILLAR 2: BENEFITS & SOLUTIONS (4 Headlines)
   - Do not mention the product name. Mention the RESULT.
   - Example: "Lower Your Monthly Bills" or "Stop Your Roof From Leaking."

3. PILLAR 3: TRUST & SOCIAL PROOF (4 Headlines)
   - Use numbers, ratings, or certifications.
   - Example: "5-Star Google Rating," "Over 10k Users," "Licensed & Insured."

4. PILLAR 4: CALL TO ACTION (3 Headlines)
   - Use strong, varied verbs: "Get," "Book," "Start," "Claim," "Download."

5. DESCRIPTIONS (4 Total):
   - Desc 1: {KeyWord:Default} + USP + CTA.
   - Desc 2: Problem + Solution + Trust Signal.
   - Desc 3: Offer-focused (e.g., "Save 20% today when you...") + Urgency.
   - Desc 4: Narrative style focused on the user's "After State" (success).

### CRITICAL GUARDRAILS:
- NO TRUNCATION: Every line must be a complete thought. If it's over the limit, rewrite it.
- DIVERSITY CHECK: No two headlines should start with the same 3 words.
- CHARACTER LIMITS: Strict 30 for headlines, 90 for descriptions.
- CLEANING: Strip all [brackets] or "quotes" from keywords before inserting into copy.

### OUTPUT FORMAT:
Provide the results in a table categorized by Pillar.

Key Improvement for your Builder: "The Character Buffer"
Standard AI prompts often fail because they try to hit exactly 30 characters, which can break if Google's font rendering is wider.
My logic uses the "27-Character Target": I’ve instructed the AI to aim for slightly shorter headlines. This is the secret to getting a "clean" look on mobile devices and higher click-through rates (CTR).
